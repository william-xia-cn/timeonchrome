Privacy Policy for TimeOnChrome

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

TimeOnChrome is a non-commercial, open-source browser extension designed for families to help manage and monitor web usage. Your privacy is our priority.

1. Data Collection and Usage

TimeOnChrome collects and processes the following data:

  • Browsing Activity: The extension monitors the URLs of websites visited to track time usage and apply filtering rules. This data is handled locally.
  • User Settings: Your customized blocking rules and preferences.

2. Data Storage and Syncing

By default, all data is stored locally within your browser's storage. If you enable the optional Cloud Sync feature (via Cloudflare Workers), your settings and usage logs are encrypted and synchronized to your private storage account. We do not have access to your raw data.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to outside parties. This extension is strictly non-commercial and does not contain any tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics scripts.

4. Permissions Disclosure

To perform its core functions, TimeOnChrome requires the following permissions:

  • <all_urls>: Necessary to detect website visits across all domains for time tracking and filtering.
  • Storage: To save your local preferences and rules.
  • Management: (If applicable) To ensure the extension functions as a management tool.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our GitHub repository.
